# Micro and Macro receipts

[Specification home](../README.md) · [Evidence index](../index.md) · [Entercation ecosystem](https://github.com/entercation2021-hue/Entercation-AI-Frameworks)

## From an individual record to a delivered package

1. **Micro.** An individual event or submission produces an evidence object. Its identifier and digest refer to that specific record under the applicable encoding rules.
2. **Macro.** An organization assembles an aggregate for a defined scope and reporting period. Where the package includes Micro references or digests, a reviewer can trace the reported set to the referenced records, subject to access permissions.
3. **Delivery.** Outerprise Package Delivery (OPD) transfers the identified package through an approved channel. The recipient can compare delivered bytes against the expected digest and record its receipt or disposition.
4. **Review.** Reconcile available Micro references against the Macro's stated scope and count. Report anything missing, inaccessible, duplicated, or outside scope. A digest match verifies the bytes against the reference. Evidence of completeness, consent, authority, and factual accuracy comes from the relevant records and procedures.

Keep participant-level records under the applicable access rules when sending an organizational aggregate. Use a synthetic or appropriately authorized packet for worked examples.
